### Verification and acceptance

- Provider outage/timeout/crash does not lose enquiries or immutable quotes; unknown delivery is visible and not blindly duplicated. Bounce/complaint suppresses future invalid sends; forged/replayed callbacks cannot alter state.
- Demo never sends to arbitrary real email/number or enables payment. Callback fixtures cannot create a paid outcome from redirect or wrong amount/merchant.
- Restricted export, holds, resumable deletion and restore deletion replay preserve scope and privacy; tracking refusal leaves the form functional.
- Trace demonstrates request-to-worker linkage; automated log inspection confirms no customer text, secrets, tokens, presigned URLs or webhook bodies, including errors. Public users cannot reach metrics/dashboards.
- Trigger backlog/error/backup alerts in a controlled environment and record actual receiver evidence where available; distinguish configured from delivered.
